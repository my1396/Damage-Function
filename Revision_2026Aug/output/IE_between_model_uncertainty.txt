--- Do the two paired routes agree? (90% width of IE_2100) ---

AFE is the only estimator that can be both refit and drawn analytically,
so it is the only place the analytic route can be checked. 'AFE ratio' is
analytic / resampled: near 1 means the joint covariance reproduces what
actually refitting the models gives, which is the evidence that the IFE
bands -- where no refit is possible -- can be trusted.
The ratio comes in below 1: the analytic route imposes normality on c_hat
while resampling picks up its heavier tails, the same effect 9-3 found
when the country-resampled coefficient sd came in 1.1-1.6x the analytic
clustered se. Read the IFE bands as, if anything, slightly narrow.
The V_bai columns re-run the analytic route with Bai's heteroskedastic
meat instead of the clustered one, as a sensitivity on that choice.
'indep infl' is what the 90% width becomes if the M4 draws are shuffled
against the M8 draws, i.e. if the pairing is thrown away.
