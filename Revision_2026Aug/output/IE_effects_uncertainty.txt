BOOTSTRAP UNCERTAINTY OF THE INTERACTIVE CONTRIBUTION (IE-effects)
==================================================================

Scenario: SSP585 | spec: Interactive | draws: 1000 | countries: 149 
IE-effects_t = delta^All_t - delta^Dir_t, differenced within each draw.
delta^Dir applies the same drawn coefficients with the four interaction
terms set to zero. A POSITIVE value means interactions MITIGATE damages.
Coefficients drawn from N(c_hat, V_hat) and mapped to beta_0..beta_L.
Units: percentage points of the 2100 GDP ratio.

sd(IE)/sd(All) compares the spread of the interactive contribution with
the spread of delta^All itself. It exceeds 1 at L = 2 because the two
compounded pathways diverge there: the difference of two paths is not
bounded by their levels.
