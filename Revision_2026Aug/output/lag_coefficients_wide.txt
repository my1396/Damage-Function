DISTRIBUTED-LAG COEFFICIENTS IN THE ORIGINAL PARAMETERIZATION
=============================================================

Model:  y_t = sum_{j=0}^{L} beta_j' x_{t-j} + country/year FE + country trends
beta_0 = contemporaneous, beta_1 = one-year lag, beta_2 = two-year lag
SUM    = cumulative effect (this is what the projection scales with)

AFE = additive country+year FE, HC0 SE clustered by country
IFE = interactive FE, Bai (2009), 4 factors
Significance: * p<0.10  ** p<0.05  *** p<0.01
Standard errors in parentheses (delta method).
