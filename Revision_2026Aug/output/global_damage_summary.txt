BETWEEN-MODEL INTERACTIVE CONTRIBUTION: POINT ESTIMATES
======================================================

IE = delta^M8 - delta^M4, the difference between the two SEPARATELY
ESTIMATED models: M = 8 adds the four T x P interactions to the M = 4
direct terms. Contrast 9_decompose_IE_effects.R, which measures the
WITHIN-model gap by zeroing the interaction coefficients inside the M = 8
fit -- a counterfactual built from coefficients never estimated without
the interactions present. The two differ sharply at L = 2.
A POSITIVE value means the interactive model projects milder damages.

Scenario: SSP585 | BHM aggregation | L = 0, 1, 2.
Levels are % of counterfactual GDP; differences are percentage points.
Point estimates only. 8-1_IE_between_model_uncertainty.R attaches the
bootstrap bands and reproduces the IE column below exactly.
