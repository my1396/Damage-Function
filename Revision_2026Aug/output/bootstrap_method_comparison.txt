============================================================

Country resampling : 122 countries drawn with replacement, each drawn
  copy given its own fixed effect and its own linear and quadratic
  trends, AFE refit on the resampled panel. Makes no distributional
  assumption and regenerates the country clustering directly.
Coefficient resampling : c ~ N(c_hat, V_hat) from the full-sample fit,
  no refitting. The method used in 7_ and 9-2, and the only feasible
  one for IFE. Imposes normality and takes V_hat as known.

Draws: 1000 per method. Dropped for collinearity: L=0: 0, L=1: 0, L=2: 0 
Both methods share the point estimate; only the bands differ.
Units: percentage points of the 2100 GDP ratio.
component All = delta^All_2100, IE = delta^All_2100 - delta^Dir_2100.
width ratio = 90% width relative to coefficient resampling.
sd is not reported: a handful of explosive draws dominate it (see the
second table), so the quantile-based width is the meaningful comparison.

--- Where the two methods diverge: the delta^Dir counterfactual ---

delta^Dir zeroes the four interaction coefficients and compounds the
remaining direct terms for 80 years. When a draw puts those terms in the
positive-growth region the path explodes, and since IE = All - Dir the
explosion shows up as a large NEGATIVE IE. max Dir (x) is the largest
delta^Dir_2100 in the sample, as a multiple of counterfactual GDP.

--- The within-draw pairing, and what it is worth ---

delta^Dir is not a separate estimate: ie_path() derives it from the SAME
beta as delta^All by zeroing the four interaction columns, so every draw
is paired by construction and no cross-model covariance has to be
recovered. (8-1 does have to recover one, because there the M = 8 and
M = 4 models are separately estimated.) 'shuffled' differences delta^All
against a random permutation of delta^Dir, which leaves both marginals
untouched, so 'pairing worth' is the factor by which the 90% width would
inflate if the pairing were thrown away.
