JOINT SAMPLING COVARIANCE OF THE M = 8 AND M = 4 COEFFICIENTS
=============================================================

Both models are fitted to the same panel, so their coefficient vectors are
correlated. Stacking the two models' per-country scores and sandwiching
with the block-diagonal bread recovers the cross block that
lagged_climate_fits.rds does not store.

V_clu : clustered meat, sum_i s_i s_i'. Arbitrary serial correlation.
V_bai : Bai's heteroskedastic meat. No serial correlation.

dev_coef_* is the largest absolute deviation of the refitted coefficients
from those stored by 3_lagged_climate.R; it must be ~0 or the refit here
is not the same fit whose covariance is being reported.
dev_clu_*/dev_bai_* is the largest absolute deviation of a DIAGONAL block
from the vcov stored by 3_lagged_climate.R. Expected ~0 for
AFE/V_clu (3_ uses vcovHC(cluster = 'group')) and for IFE/V_bai (3_ uses
Bai's heteroskedastic form); the other two are genuinely different
robustness assumptions and are not expected to match.
min_eig > 0 confirms V_clu is positive definite.
