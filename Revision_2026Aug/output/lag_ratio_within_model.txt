PERSISTENCE RATIO: CUMULATIVE / IMPACT MARGINAL EFFECT
=====================================================

rho = [dg/dT at sum_j beta_j] / [dg/dT at beta_0], evaluated at the
median precipitation and the stated temperature percentile.

rho = 1  growth effect : the shock never unwinds
rho = 0  level  effect : the lags exactly offset the impact effect

se_ratio is the delta method applied to the ratio of two linear forms in
the same Bewley coefficient vector, using the full covariance matrix, so
the correlation between numerator and denominator is carried.
lo/hi are rho -/+ 1.96 x se_ratio.

--- Reading ---

All 40 of 40 point estimates fall inside [0, 1], between a pure level
effect and a pure growth effect. The median is 0.62 and the mean 0.58, so
the central estimate is that roughly 62% of the impact effect survives
once the lags have played out -- closer to a growth effect than to a level
effect, but not cleanly either.

The bands are too wide to settle it. 40 of 40 cover rho = 1, 32 cover
rho = 0, and 32 cover BOTH, which is to say most rows are consistent with
either extreme. Standard errors rise steeply with L (see the by-lag-length
block): adding lag terms buys generality at a real cost in precision.

Only 8 rows rule out a pure level effect (lo > 0): IFE L=1, IFE L=2.
Those are the short-lag IFE fits, the most precisely estimated cells.
No row rules out a pure growth effect.
